About

I’m an offensive security engineer with a background in software development and QA automation. I focus on web applications, APIs, and business logic flaws, and I build AI-assisted tools for security testing.

Having worked as a tester, a developer, and an attacker, I look at applications from all three perspectives: how they should work, how they are built, and how someone could misuse them. I’m especially interested in how seemingly valid actions can combine to produce an outcome the business never intended.

I like turning those questions into working tools and testing what actually happens. This is where I share my personal projects, research, and experiments.
