About

My superpower is looking at the same application as a tester, a developer, and an attacker.

I've worked in all three roles, so I can connect how something should work, how it was built, and how someone could misuse it. That's why I see myself as a connector.

I help teams find security problems that become visible when you look at the whole process. Each feature might work correctly on its own, but what happens when someone combines actions in an unexpected way? I investigate that and show what actually happens.

I'm a Red Team Engineer building AI-assisted security tools and exploring the threads connecting security, AI, and software engineering.
